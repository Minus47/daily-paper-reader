## 摘要
立体脑电图（SEEG）是一种侵入性技术，用于耐药性癫痫的术前评估，以支持致痫区（EZ）的定位。SEEG数据分析尤为复杂，因为它包含数百个成分（称为通道），每个通道对应不同的脑区，并且其相互作用在时间和频率上均会变化。在本研究中，我们提出了一种基于Canonical Polyadic（CP）分解的SEEG分析新框架，用于提取癫痫发作生成背后的潜在模式。我们将SEEG信号建模为三阶张量（通道×时间×频率），并将该张量分解为秩1因子。随后，我们利用通道特定因子作为无监督特征集来识别EZ。该框架在35例耐药性癫痫患者的SEEG记录发作数据上进行了验证。结果表明，多个频带的联合组合显著提升了分类性能。

## Abstract
Stereo-electroencephalography (SEEG) is an invasive technique used in the presurgical evaluation of drug-resistant epilepsy to support the localization of the Epileptogenic Zone (EZ). SEEG data are particularly complex to analyze, as they include hundreds of components, named channels, each associated with a different brain site, with interactions varying in both time and frequency. In this study, we propose a new framework for SEEG analysis, based on the Canonical Polyadic (CP) decomposition, to extract the latent modes underlying seizure generation. We model SEEG signals as a third-order tensor (channel x time x frequency), and factorize the tensor into rank-1 factors. We then exploit the channel-specific factor as an unsupervised feature set for identifying the EZ. The framework is validated on SEEG-recorded seizures from 35 patients with drug-resistant epilepsy. The results show that the joint combination of multiple frequency bands significantly enhances classification performance.
