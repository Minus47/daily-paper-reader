## 摘要
脑电图（EEG）研究越来越多地采用缺乏离散事件时间信息的自然情境与静息态范式。这些记录包含结构化的振荡活动，但通常缺乏重复的刺激起始点，无法在试次或被试之间定义共享的时间基准。我们提出 EEGSync，一种面向带限解析 EEG 信号的复值酉 Procrustes 时间对齐方法。EEGSync 将每个被试表示为一个由复振荡轨迹构成的矩阵，并求解酉 Procrustes 问题，将目标被试映射到参考模板或组模板的时间坐标系中。该方法通过经济型 SVD 分解实现计算可行性，避免了构造完整的时间算子。这种对齐严格保持 EEG 通道间原有的成对关系和功率。该酉变换对齐的是潜在时间结构，而非估计物理时钟偏移。因此，相位锁定值、振幅包络相关和相位-振幅耦合等非线性瞬时指标并不保证具有不变性。这一框架使 EEGSync 非常适合自然情境下的共享刺激数据，并可能作为保持协方差的潜在流形对齐方法用于静息态数据。在生理和临床场景中通过被试独立交叉验证评估，基于 EEGSync 的特征在睁眼与闭眼状态判别中达到 0.9600 的 AUROC 和 89.45% 的最高分类准确率。在阿尔茨海默病与健康对照分类中，闭眼记录的最高 AUROC 达到 0.9272，并在睁眼光刺激条件下进一步实现判别。这些发现表明，时间对齐捕获了可补充传统频谱与统计 EEG 特征的可判别信息。

## Abstract
Electroencephalography (EEG) studies increasingly use naturalistic and resting-state paradigms that lack discrete event timing. These recordings contain structured oscillatory activity but often lack repeated stimulus onsets to define a shared time clock across trials or participants. We introduce EEGSync, a complex-valued unitary Procrustes temporal alignment method for band-limited analytic EEG signals. EEGSync represents each subject as a matrix of complex oscillatory trajectories and solves a unitary Procrustes problem, mapping a target subject into the temporal coordinate system of a reference or group template. The method is computationally tractable through an economy-SVD factorization that avoids constructing the full temporal operator. This alignment strictly preserves original pairwise relationships and power across EEG channels. The unitary transformation aligns latent temporal structure rather than estimating a physical clock offset. Consequently, nonlinear instantaneous measures like phase-locking value, amplitude-envelope correlation, and phase-amplitude coupling are not guaranteed to be invariant. This framing makes EEGSync highly suitable for naturalistic shared-stimulus data, and potentially useful for resting-state data as a covariance-preserving latent manifold alignment.   Evaluated via subject-independent cross-validation across physiological and clinical settings, EEGSync-based features achieved an AUROC of 0.9600 and 89.45% maximum classification accuracy in eyes-open versus eyes-closed state discrimination. For Alzheimer's disease versus healthy control classification, the highest AUROC reached 0.9272 in eyes-closed recordings, with further discrimination under eyes-open photostimulation. These findings suggest temporal alignment captures discriminative information complementing conventional spectral and statistical EEG features.
