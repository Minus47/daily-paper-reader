## 摘要
从脑电图（EEG）解码视觉体验的神经解码在被试内设置中已取得强性能，但当前方法无法跨个体泛化：在标准 200 类基准 THINGS-EEG-2 上，top-1 准确率从被试内的 80% 以上下降到未见被试的约 32%。将预训练编码器适配到新被试和新会话通常需要标注数据，而标注数据的采集时间这一瓶颈严重限制了设备的实际部署。然而，在实际的脑机接口（BCI）中，可能的结果事先已知并构成候选库，并且用户的无标签 EEG 会随着设备使用而不断累积。我们提出一种新设置——增量转导校准，其中解码器定期基于迄今收到的所有无标签 EEG 和已知候选库进行校准，无需任何标签。该设置比当前使用的零样本和批量评估更接近已部署 BCI 的条件。我们用一个协议评估它：查询逐个到达，其频率未知且可能不相等，并且每个查询在被用于校准之前先被解码。在此设置下，我们提出 GALA：GAllery-Level Alignment（候选库级对齐），一种无标签校准方法；它通过收缩白化、hubness 校正以及带查询频率估计的 Sinkhorn–Procrustes 对齐，将无标签 EEG 嵌入配准到候选库上，其中查询频率从缓冲区估计，并在每次查询后更新。在 THINGS-EEG-2 上，当候选库包含 200 张图像时，它在 53 分钟使用时间内将新被试的 top-1 准确率从 28% 提升到 67%。在改变候选库大小、查询频率以及每个查询平均重复次数的 60 种评估设置中，GALA 在每种设置下都是最准确的无标签方法；在数据流结束时，其平均准确率比最佳对比方法高 6.6 个百分点。

## Abstract
Neural decoding of visual experience from electroencephalography (EEG) has achieved strong performance in intra-subject settings, but current methods fail to generalize across individuals: on the standard 200-way benchmark THINGS-EEG-2, top-1 accuracy drops from above 80% within subjects to about 32% for an unseen subject. Adapting a pre-trained encoder to new subjects and sessions typically requires labeled data, a bottleneck whose collection time severely limits practical device deployment. In a practical Brain-Computer Interface (BCI), however, the possible outcomes are known in advance and form a gallery of candidates, and the unlabeled EEG of the user accumulates as soon as the device is used. We propose a new setting, incremental transductive calibration, in which the decoder is calibrated regularly on all the unlabeled EEG received so far and on the known gallery, without any labels. This setting is closer to the conditions of a deployed BCI than the zero-shot and batch evaluations in current use. We evaluate it with a protocol in which queries arrive one at a time, with unknown and possibly unequal frequencies, and each query is decoded before it is used for calibration. In this setting, we introduce GALA : GAllery-Level Alignment, a label-free calibration method which registers the unlabeled EEG embeddings onto the gallery through shrinkage whitening, hubness correction and a Sinkhorn--Procrustes alignment with query frequencies estimated from the buffer, updated after every query. On THINGS-EEG-2, with a gallery of 200 images, it raises the top-1 accuracy on a new subject from 28% to 67% over 53 minutes of use. Across 60 evaluation settings that vary the gallery size, the query frequencies and the number of repetitions averaged per query, GALA is the most accurate label-free method in every setting, 6.6 points above the best competing method on average at the end of the stream.
