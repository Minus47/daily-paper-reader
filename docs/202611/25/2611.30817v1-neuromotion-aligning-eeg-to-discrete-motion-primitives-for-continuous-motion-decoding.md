## 摘要
EEG 到运动的解码仍然具有挑战性，因为含噪的头皮 EEG 难以直接映射为连续运动学参数。我们提出 NeuroMotion，一个将 EEG 表征对齐到离散运动基元的框架，用于跨异构范式的连续运动解码，包括全身自由运动、下肢跑步机行走以及上肢自主伸够与抓握。基于 VQ 的运动先验将连续运动离散化为可复用的基元，这些基元既作为对齐原型，也作为中间预测目标。原型对比目标和监督对比目标将 EEG 嵌入对齐到由运动导出的原型，并根据基元分配对其进行组织。E2M Transformer 从学习到的 EEG 表征预测运动码序列，而最终的几何精化阶段通过运动级训练改进连续轨迹重建。在末次会话测试协议下，NeuroMotion 在所有三个数据集上于所比较的 EEG 解码基线中取得最佳平均重建性能，并在全身运动上于重建准确率和分布级质量两方面均取得最明显提升。多被试消融进一步支持了 EEG 到基元对齐、token 预测和精化阶段的贡献。代码可在 https://github.com/Liuyi2351433/NeuroAction 获取。

## Abstract
EEG-to-motion decoding remains challenging because noisy scalp EEG is difficult to map directly to continuous kinematics. We present NeuroMotion, a framework that aligns EEG representations to discrete motion primitives for continuous motion decoding across heterogeneous paradigms, including full-body free movement, lower-limb treadmill locomotion, and upper-limb freewill reaching and grasping. A VQ-based motion prior discretizes continuous motion into reusable primitives that serve as both alignment prototypes and intermediate prediction targets. Prototypical and supervised contrastive objectives align EEG embeddings to motion-derived prototypes and organize them according to primitive assignments. An E2M Transformer predicts motion-code sequences from the learned EEG representations, while a final geometric refinement stage improves continuous trajectory reconstruction through motion-level training. Under a last-session test protocol, NeuroMotion achieves the best mean reconstruction performance among the compared EEG decoding baselines across all three datasets, with the clearest gains on full-body motion in both reconstruction accuracy and distribution-level quality. Multi-subject ablations further support the contributions of EEG-to-primitive alignment, token prediction, and refinement. Code is available at https://github.com/Liuyi2351433/NeuroAction.
