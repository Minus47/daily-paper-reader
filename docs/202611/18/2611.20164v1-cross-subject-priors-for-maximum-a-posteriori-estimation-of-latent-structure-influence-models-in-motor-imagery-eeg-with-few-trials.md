## 摘要
耦合隐马尔可夫模型（如潜在结构影响模型，LSIM）描述了运动想象期间两个半球的感觉运动节律如何演变和相互作用，但当可用试次较少时，被试特定的最大似然（ML）估计不稳定。本文推导了LSIM的最大后验（MAP）估计器，其共轭先验等于来自拟合于其他被试的群体模型的$λ$个伪试次的期望统计量。M步保持闭式，期望最大化保持单调上升，而ML（$λ=0$）和群体模型（$λ\to\infty$）是极限情况。先验强度在PhysioNet脑电运动/想象数据集的35名被试上选择，并原样应用于70名留出被试以及BCI Competition IV-2a。当$λ=1$时，留出对数似然在70名被试中的61名上超过ML（Holm校正$p=9.1\times10^{-8}$），并在每类五个训练试次时在全部70名被试上超过ML。仅从PhysioNet数据学习到的先验在每类五个试次时提高了全部九名BCI IV-2a被试的留出似然，并将联合转移矩阵的误差降低了61%。解码准确率没有提高（$p=0.17$）。因此，跨被试先验稳定了少试次运动想象脑电的动态模型，但并未改善解码，因为这些数据中的解码主要依赖于跨被试共享的结构。

## Abstract
Coupled hidden Markov models such as the latent structure influence model (LSIM) describe how the sensorimotor rhythms of the two hemispheres evolve and interact during motor imagery, but subject-specific maximum likelihood (ML) estimates are unstable when few trials are available. This paper derives a maximum a posteriori (MAP) estimator for the LSIM whose conjugate prior equals the expected statistics of $λ$ pseudo-trials from a population model fitted to other subjects. The M-step remains in closed form, expectation maximization keeps its monotone ascent, and ML ($λ=0$) and the population model ($λ\to\infty$) are limiting cases. The prior strength was selected on 35 subjects of the PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset and applied unchanged to 70 held-out subjects and to BCI Competition IV-2a. With $λ=1$ the held-out log-likelihood exceeded that of ML in 61 of 70 subjects (Holm-adjusted $p=9.1\times10^{-8}$) and in all 70 subjects with five training trials per class. A prior learned only from the PhysioNet data improved the held-out likelihood of all nine BCI IV-2a subjects at five trials per class and reduced the error of the joint transition matrix by 61%. Decoding accuracy did not improve ($p=0.17$). Cross-subject priors therefore stabilize few-trial dynamic models of motor-imagery EEG, but they do not improve decoding, which in these data relies mainly on structure shared across subjects.
